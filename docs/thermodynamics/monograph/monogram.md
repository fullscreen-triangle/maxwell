### Part II — Dissolution of the Classical Paradoxes
**Papers:** 2, 3, 4 (Gibbs, Maxwell's Demon, Kelvin)

Three paradoxes have blocked thermodynamics for 150 years. All three dissolve through the same move: they assumed that thermodynamic evolution is driven by kinetic sorting (who is fast, who is slow, where molecules are). It is not. **Gibbs paradox** (paper 2): Mixing entropy does not depend on whether molecules are "distinguishable." **Kelvin's heat death** (paper 4): Heat death is not stasis. It is a phase transition from kinetic dynamics to categorical dynamics. ### Part III — The Categorical Thermodynamic Framework
